# Theory

The model behind LoopSage: loop extrusion, the energy function, Monte Carlo sampling and the 3D polymer.

- [Scope](scope.md): what the model should explain, and how a few rules produce the patterns of a contact map.
- [Loop extrusion](loop-extrusion.md): the molecular players (cohesin, CTCF, condensin), the loop extrusion model with its assumptions, TADs and contact maps, stripes and why dynamics is needed.
- [Mechanics](mechanics.md): Hamiltonian, phase space, ensembles, the Boltzmann law, and why an energy-based model is a good approximation for a cell that is not in equilibrium.
- [MCMC](mcmc.md): Markov chains, detailed balance, ergodicity, ergodic means, burn-in and autocorrelation.
- [Molecular dynamics](molecular-dynamics.md): the polymer, the force field, Newton's second law and the Langevin equation.
- [Topology](topology.md): knots, links and the linking number, how it is computed in 3D, and how loops are checked for linking (optional, advanced).
